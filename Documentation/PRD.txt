App name: DinePosAi
One-line app idea: 
Target users: Restaurant Managers and Restaurant Owners
Problem you are solving: Managing restaurant orders, organizing restaurant workflow, keeping track of work and data reports.
Main features: Ai base suggestion system
User roles: Super Admin, Admin (Manager), Cashier, Kitchen Staff, and Customers
MVP scope
